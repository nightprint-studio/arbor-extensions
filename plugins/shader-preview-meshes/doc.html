Preview meshes

Four shapes for looking at a material: a sphere (every surface normal at once), a cube (a hard normal discontinuity), a plane (no geometry in the way of a shader that only writes colour) and a torus — the cheapest shape with both curvature signs, so a term that reads right on a sphere and wrong in a saddle shows the difference there and nowhere else.

The sphere and the torus take a resolution between 1 and 4. Turn it up when a shader perturbs normals and you want more geometry for it to work with.

Your own geometry

Build a second mesh-source package from your engine's own crates and drop it in your plugins folder — it appears in the picker beside these. It never has to be published: a package is a folder with a plugin.toml, and the registry is how other people find one, not how Arbor loads one.

What it can reach

Nothing. No network, no filesystem, no credentials.
